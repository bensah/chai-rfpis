How RFPIS works

Read this first. It explains where opportunities come from and what you do with them.

RFPIS finds funding opportunities (RFPs, RFIs, EOIs, calls for proposals and grand challenges) on donor websites and the open web. It checks each one against your organisation's eligibility rules, and helps the team decide to Proceed, Park or Decline.

Where opportunities come from

Collecting opportunities and screening them are two separate steps.

Super User
Extraction The platform's Super User runs a separate crawl of every donor source and pulls new RFPs into a shared store.
Automatic
Friday scan Every Friday at 06:00 UTC a scheduled scan crawls the sources again and screens each organisation.
You get alerted
Raw records ready The 🔔 bell shows "Scan completed" when new raw records are in the store.
You
Eligibility Scan Click 🎯 Eligibility Scan to pull the calls that fit your organisation into your pipeline.
The team
Review & decide Proceed, Park or Decline, then track deadlines and follow-ups.
Raw records vs. your pipeline Raw records are every call the crawl found, from every country and theme, before any screening. They sit in a shared, platform-wide pool. The Eligibility Scan reads that pool against your organisation's rules and adds only the calls that fit to your pipeline. A call that any user submits joins the same pool. Other organisations can then find it on their next scan.

Signing in for the first time

Pages: /activate-account · /login · /password-reset

Nobody can sign themselves up. Your administrator creates your account, and RFPIS then emails you an invitation.

A. Activate your account

  1. Open the email titled "Activate your RFPIS APP account". It is sent to the address your admin entered, for example user@example.com.
  2. Click Activate my account. The link works for 7 days. The email also shows an activation code you can paste in instead.
  3. On the 🔑 Activate your account screen, check that the email is yours, then click Activate account.
  4. Type a new password twice and click Save password.
  5. When you see "Account activated", click Go to sign in.

B. Sign in

  1. Go to your RFPIS web address. If you aren't signed in, you land on the sign-in screen.
  2. In Username, type your email address.
  3. Type your Password and click Login.
  4. You arrive on Home. You stay signed in for about 8 hours.

C. Forgot your password?

  1. On the sign-in screen, open 🔐 Forgot password?.
  2. Type your email and click 📨 Request password reset.
  3. An administrator approves the request. You then get an email titled "Change your RFPIS APP password". Your old password keeps working until you use the new link.
  4. Click Set a new password in the email. Or paste its code into 🔑 Have an activation or reset code?. The code expires in 2 hours.
If an admin reset your password The first time you sign in, RFPIS asks you to choose your own password. Enter the temporary password, then your new one twice, and click 🔐 Save new password.

Run the Eligibility Scan

Sidebar → 📚 Pipelines → Screen tab. Every team member can do this.

The Eligibility Scan reads the shared store of raw records against your organisation's eligibility rules. It adds the calls that fit to your pipeline. It doesn't crawl the web, so it takes seconds.

  1. When the 🔔 bell shows "Scan completed", or your administrator tells you new records are ready, open 📚 Pipelines.
  2. Stay on the Screen tab. At the top right, beside 📝 Submit New Funding, click 🎯 Eligibility Scan.
  3. Wait while the button shows ⏳ Scanning…. Don't refresh the page.
  4. Read the green banner at the top. For example: "Screened 120 curated solicitations → 8 eligible for your org (5 newly added, 3 already in your pipeline) · 112 not a fit."
  5. Scroll down the Screen tab to see the new opportunities for this review week.
Run it as often as you like Calls already in your pipeline aren't added twice. The result banner tells you how many were already there.
Extraction is different SUPER USER The full Extraction crawls every donor site and takes 20–40 minutes. Only the platform's Super User runs it, from Settings → Manual Scan → ⛏ Extraction. The automatic Friday scan does the same job on a schedule. You never need to run it.

Read the Screen tab

Sidebar → 📚 Pipelines → Screen. This is the week's list, which the team walks through on the Monday call.

  1. Pick the Review week at the top. It defaults to the current week.
  2. Optional: open Filters to narrow by Decision, Feasibility, Geographic scope or Program area.
  3. Read the counters: Total Screened, Proceed, Parked and Declined. Under them you'll see the Largest Opportunity, the Nearest Deadline, and the Prime and Sub opportunities.
  4. Scroll to Proceed & Parked RFPs — Rationale & Risks. Each row shows the score, the system's suggestion (Auto-rec), the team's Decision, key risks and confidence.
  5. To discuss one opportunity in depth, go to the Review tab.
Empty week? If you see "No RFPs recorded for this week", click the Jump to … button. It takes you to the latest week that has records.

Review an opportunity and update the decision

Sidebar → 📚 Pipelines → Review tab. Every team member can update a decision.

How the score works

Every opportunity is scored against nine criteria. The five MUSTs make up 65% of the score: legal status, strategic fit, implementation capacity, geographic fit, and co-financing & compliance. The four PREFERs make up 35%: funding quality, donor relationship, competitiveness and bid effort.

ProceedScore of 90 or above
Park70 to 89: worth a human look
DeclineBelow 70, or a fatal eligibility gate

Review an opportunity

  1. Open the Review tab. Choose the Review week, then pick the RFP from the RFPs in <week> list.
  2. Read the cards across the top: Key details, Value, Focus Areas, Geographic Scope and Brief description.
  3. Look at the Bid Strength gauge, the System suggestion and the Confidence.
  4. Open any criterion in the Eligibility criteria grid, for example "MUST 4 · Geographic fit", to see why it was met or not met.

Update the decision

  1. Scroll to Team decision and click ✏ Update Decision.
  2. Optional: correct any criterion component the system got wrong. The score and suggestion follow your changes. A person who has read the call outranks the system.
  3. Set Decline flags? (No / Yes) and write the Key risks in one line.
  4. Choose the Decision: Proceed, Park or Decline.
  5. Write the Decision rationale in two or three lines.
  6. Answer Donor already engaged on this opportunity? with Yes, Partial or No.
  7. Click 💾 Save changes. A confirmation shows the new score, suggestion and decision.
More edits: ✏️ Edit RFP Beside Update Decision, ✏️ Edit RFP opens the full record. Its tabs are Opportunity, Eligibility, Decision & Pipeline, Team, Submission & Award and Application. Use it to set the Proposal lead, Assigned to, Next action and Action deadline.
Deleting an RFP ADMIN ONLY The 🗑 Delete this RFP button inside Edit RFP works for administrators only. If something shouldn't be in the pipeline, Decline it instead.

At the bottom of the Review tab, rate the RFP with 👍 Good, 😐 Neutral or 👎 Bad. Your ratings teach the scoring model what your team values.

Track and Summary

Sidebar → 📚 Pipelines → Track and Summary tabs.

Track: opportunities you're pursuing

  1. Open the Track tab. It lists every active Proceed RFP for the year.
  2. Pick one in Select RFP to review, or click its row in the table.
  3. Check Days to deadline, Stage, Progress status and Proposal lead(s). The Next action comes from the meeting notes.
  4. Use 🚀 Apply on the funder's portal ↗ to go to the funder's application site.
  5. Click ✏️ Edit to update the stage, progress or next action.

Summary: the year at a glance

The Summary tab has five sections. A is the Funding Calls Snapshot, B is Pipeline health and C is Proposal development status. D is the Periodic Reflection (pick a week, month or quarter) and E is Partner engagement KRs. It's read-only and good for quarterly check-ins.

The Opportunity page

Click any opportunity title: in the Live Opportunity Feed, a bell item, search results, or 🔍 View opportunity on Review.

  1. Read the chips under the title. They show the deadline status, the type of call, and whether it's In your pipeline, Screened — not in a pipeline yet, or Shared catalogue — not screened for you.
  2. Use Open the call ↗ and Apply ↗ to go to the funder's own pages.
  3. Read 1 · The opportunity: award value, deadline, duration, overview and documents.
  4. Read 2 · Decision aid: how the call fares against your organisation, its Bid Strength, and What's against it.
  5. At the bottom:
    • For an opportunity already in your pipeline, click ✏️ Open in Review.
    • For one from the shared catalogue, click ➕ Add to my pipeline, or ✕ Not relevant to dismiss it.

Search for opportunities

Top bar → 🔍 Search, or the full page at /search.

One search box looks in two places. First it searches inside RFPIS: pages, tabs, opportunities and donors. Then, if you ask it to, it searches the web for live, recent funding calls.

A. Search inside RFPIS

  1. Click 🔍 in the top bar.
  2. Type at least two characters, for example malaria or a donor's name, and click Search. For more room, click Open full search page.
  3. Results are grouped under 📑 Pages & tabs, 📄 Opportunities and 🗺️ Donors. Open a group and click a result to go to it.

B. Search the web for new calls

  1. On the search page, scroll to 🌐 Opportunities on the web.
  2. Click 🌐 Search the web for "…".
  3. Browse the results, 15 per page, with ‹ Prev and Next ›. Rate them with 👍 😐 👎 to improve future results.
  4. Found a real opportunity? Click ➕ Track. RFPIS scores it and adds it to your pipeline. It tells you if the call is already tracked.
"Web search isn't configured yet"? Web search needs a key that an administrator sets up. Ask them to enable it.

Submit an RFP you found elsewhere

Pipelines → Screen → 📝 Submit New Funding, or go to /submit-new-rfp. Every team member can do this.

The scans don't catch everything. A colleague might forward a call, or a donor might publish it on a partner's website. When you find an RFP that isn't in RFPIS yet, submit it here. It goes straight into your pipeline and joins the shared pool for everyone else.

Check first Search for the title or donor with 🔍 before submitting. It may already be in RFPIS.
  1. Go to 📚 Pipelines → Screen and click 📝 Submit New Funding.
  2. Section 1 · Opportunity description. Fill in the Opportunity title * and the Donor *. If the donor isn't listed, choose Other and type its name. Add the deadline, the Opportunity link (URL), the estimated value and currency, and the Lead applicant *.
  3. Section 2 · Eligibility. For each of the nine MUST / PREFER questions, pick your best answer. Leave it as Not sure if you don't know.
  4. Section 3 · Decline flags & risks. Answer Decline flags present? * and write the key risks in one line.
  5. Section 4 · Initial decision. Choose your Bid decision * and add a short rationale.
  6. Section 5 · Team. This appears for Proceed or Park only. Pick the Proposal lead *, and optionally contributors and reviewers.
  7. Click Submit RFP. You'll see "✅ Submitted as UID — alignment score X/100". The RFP is tagged to this review week, ready for the team call.
Always paste the link RFPIS needs the Opportunity link (URL) to share the call with other organisations and to check it later. If the deadline has already passed, RFPIS warns you and offers 🔗 Open … to fix this.

Actions: meetings, notes and follow-ups

Sidebar → 🗒️ Actions. You'll use this page most during team meetings.

The Actions page has four tabs. Meetings holds the notes from each team call. Engagements logs contact with donors. Pending gathers every open follow-up. Schedule sets who runs each call.

A good team-meeting routine Chair: opens Pipelines → Screen for this week. Presenter: walks through each RFP on Review. Team: agrees on the decision, and someone clicks ✏ Update Decision. Note-taker: adds a note for each agreed action, with an owner and due date. Before the call ends: open Pending and close anything that's done.

A. Meetings: capture notes during the call

REVIEWER+ Adding notes and changing their status needs the Reviewer role or above. Contributors can read the notes and download them.

  1. Open 🗒️ Actions → Meetings. Check the Review week. The tiles show the Meeting date, Note-taker, RFP presenter and Chair.
  2. Click ➕ Add a note at the top right.
  3. In Linked RFP (Proceed) *, choose the opportunity being discussed. Only RFPs the team has agreed to pursue are listed.
  4. Write the Issues * raised, then the Actions / recommendations * agreed.
  5. Pick the Owner * and a Due Date *. The due date defaults to the next Monday.
  6. Click 💾 Save note. The note appears in Notes for <week>.
  7. When the action is done, click its red Not Resolved button. It turns green: ✓ Resolved.
  8. Need a copy for the minutes? Click ⬇ Download <week> notes as CSV.
Correcting a saved note You can't edit a saved note from this tab. Ask an administrator. They can change it under Settings → Records → Data → Meeting Logs.

B. Engagements: log a donor touchpoint

  1. Open Actions → Engagements. The top shows how many engagements you've logged this quarter against the 2–4 target.
  2. In Log an engagement, set the Engagement date *, Type * and Format.
  3. Choose the Donor * and the Internal lead *. Pick Other to type a name that isn't listed.
  4. Add the donor contacts, the Purpose and the Outcome / follow-up. Link it to an RFP if there is one.
  5. Click 💾 Save engagement.

C. Pending: close your open actions

  1. Open Actions → Pending. It shows every unresolved meeting action and donor follow-up, with a count by owner.
  2. Open Filters and choose your name under Owner to see only your items.
  3. When an item is done, click Not Resolved. It's marked resolved and drops off the list.

D. Schedule: who runs each call

  1. Open Actions → Schedule. Switch the View between Week, Month, Year and Day. Browse with ◀ Prev, ● Today and Next ▶.
  2. To set up a call, open ➕ Add or update a call. Pick the Call date, Note-taker, RFP presenter and Meeting chair, then click 💾 Save call.

Generate a report

Sidebar → 📊 Report.

  1. Choose the Period: This year, Year to date, a Specific month, Last 90 days, Last 12 months or All time. Pick the Year, and the Month if you chose a specific month.
  2. Choose View by: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semestrial or Annually.
  3. Optional: open ⚙️ Advanced filter to choose which sections and metrics to include. There are five sections: Scan activity, Team & partners, Insights, Reviews & decisions, and Our results.
  4. Click ▶ Generate report. Nothing loads until you do. The address bar then holds a link you can share with teammates.
  5. To download it, click 📄 Export Report, then ⬇ Download PDF. For the numbers in Excel, click 📥 Export Data.
The first PDF can be slow The first PDF export after the app restarts can take a couple of minutes. If it fails, use ⬇ Download the report as HTML instead and print that.

Grants

  1. Read the totals: Total Submitted, Approved, Under Review and Not Approved. Below them are the amounts requested and secured, in USD, and your win rate.

Your organisation (Tenant)

👤 Person menu → Tenant. In this manual, the example organisation is called "RFPIS APP".

This page is your organisation's profile. RFPIS uses it to decide which calls fit you: the countries you work in, your program areas, funders and team.

  1. Check the profile completeness bar. A fuller profile gives better matches.
  2. Read the cards: 🏷 Identity & eligibility, 💰 Capacity & funding targets, 🎯 Program areas (graded 0–5), 🌍 Geography & partners, 🤝 Funders & languages and 👥 Team members.
  3. If something is wrong or out of date, tell your administrator.
Editing the profile ADMIN ONLY Administrators see ✏️ Edit tenant. Everyone else sees "View only".

Glossary

Raw records

Every call the crawl found, from every country and theme, before screening.

Eligibility Scan

Screens raw records against your rules. Only the calls that fit join your pipeline.

Review week

The Monday-to-Sunday week an RFP is tagged to for the team call.

Proceed / Park / Decline

Pursue it / revisit it later / don't pursue it.

Bid Strength

A score from 0 to 100 across the five MUSTs and four PREFERs.

Tenant

Your organisation's own space in RFPIS, for example "RFPIS APP".
